Music Etymology

Dynamics change the volume of a piece while playing.

Music Terminology

Clefs tell you where on your instrument you play.

Andante
Poco
Andante is the slow tempo. It's used to play graceful and quiet pieces.
Poco means "little" in Italian and Spanish. It is added before dynamics to make the dynamic smaller and less prominent.
Allegro
Vivace
Allegro is the fast tempo, usually used in quick and lively pieces. It makes the piece quicker and more dynamic.
Vivace is the lively tempo. It's used similarly to Allegro, but is faster and more light, used in Beethoven and Mozart's pieces.

The scale shows you the notes on your instrument.

Moderato
Largo
Moderato is the medium tempo, which is the steady and calm tempo. It's a very controllable tempo.
Largo is one of the slowest tempos. It's a very broad tempo, used in heavy or dramatic pieces.
